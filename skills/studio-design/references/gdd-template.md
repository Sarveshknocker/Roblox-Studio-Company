# GDD — <game name>

## 1. Pitch
One sentence: *In <game>, you <verb> to <goal> in <setting>, unlike <competitor> because <hook>.*

## 2. Audience and platform
Age band, play style (solo/co-op/PvP), device split assumption (mobile-first), session length target.

## 3. Core loop
Verb → reward → upgrade → repeat, with seconds per step. Diagram (Mermaid) welcome.

## 4. The 3Cs
Character stats and abilities (numbers), camera, controls per input type.

## 5. Progression and economy
Currencies, sources and sinks, levels/rebirths, unlock list. Link `economy.json` and the latest `ECONOMY_REPORT.md`.

## 6. World and levels
Areas, unlock order, critical path, safe zones. Link greybox screenshots.

## 7. Social and multiplayer
Party/co-op rules, trading (if any: scams and duplication risks!), leaderboards, chat considerations.

## 8. UX and onboarding
Screens list, HUD, first 60 seconds script, onboarding funnel steps.

## 9. Narrative
Premise, characters, quest list.

## 10. Monetization
Passes, products, (paid random items with odds), pricing rationale, what is never sold.

## 11. Live-Ops plan
Update cadence, first three events, what data decides them.

## 12. Out of scope (for now)
The parking lot, so nobody builds it by accident.
