# G0 Kickoff interview

Ask in **one message**, numbered, with short suggested answers so the owner can reply quickly ("1b, 2: pirates, 3a…").
Offer "you decide" for every question; when the owner picks it, decide and record why.

1. **Genre** (pick one or describe your own):
   a) Obby / platformer · b) Simulator (collect, upgrade, rebirth) · c) Tycoon · d) Horror / escape · e) Roleplay / life ·
   f) Fighting / battlegrounds · g) Tower defense · h) Survival / crafting · i) Racing / vehicles · j) Puzzle / mystery ·
   k) Social hangout / party games · l) Other
2. **Story, theme or setting**: one sentence is enough ("a lighthouse keeper fighting sea monsters", "candy factory").
3. **Who is it for**: a) 9–12 · b) 13–17 · c) 17+ (needs ID-verified audience and a matching maturity label) · d) everyone.
4. **How people play**: a) solo · b) co-op with friends · c) competitive PvP · d) mix.
5. **Session length you imagine**: a) 5–10 min quick fun · b) 20–40 min · c) long sessions.
6. **Monetization comfort**: a) cosmetics only · b) cosmetics + convenience (no pay-to-win) · c) anything Roblox allows ·
   d) no monetization at first. Paid random items (loot boxes) yes/no?
7. **Scope and time**: a) small game in 2–4 weeks · b) medium in 1–3 months · c) big, long-term live game.
8. **Art style**: a) low-poly bright · b) realistic · c) stylized cartoon · d) pixel/blocky classic Roblox · e) you decide.
9. **Must-haves and no-gos**: anything you definitely want or do not want.
10. **Reference games** you like (on Roblox or elsewhere), and what you like about them.

## From answers to three concepts
1. Record the answers verbatim in `studio/00-owner/KICKOFF.md`.
2. Commission `studio-research`: genre market scan and the top competitors' loops (see that skill).
3. Write three concepts that each satisfy the answers but differ in their hook:
   - **Safe bet**: proven loop of the genre with one clear twist from the theme.
   - **Bold**: an unusual mechanic built from the story or setting.
   - **Small and polished**: the smallest version that could be excellent within the time budget.
4. For each: one-line pitch, core loop (verb → reward → upgrade → repeat), what is new versus the top 3 competitors,
   session flow, monetization that fits the owner's limits, scope estimate, biggest risk.
5. Recommend one, say why, and ask the owner to pick or mix. Record the choice in `DECISIONS.md`.
